Do a final audit and refinement of the EXISTING Hockey Heart Initiative donation and payment system.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT redesign the website.

The current HHI preview is already good.

Only fix, improve and verify the donation/payment functionality described below.

==================================================

1. USD-FIRST DONATION EXPERIENCE
    ==================================================

USD must be the primary donation currency.

The donation interface should prominently display:

$50
$100
$250
$500
Custom Amount

Minimum donation:

$50 USD equivalent.

The overall donation experience should feel international and should not look Nigeria-focused.

The primary audience is expected to be from:

* United States
* Canada
* Europe
* other international donors

Do not make Nigerian Naira the primary currency.

Allow other international currencies only where genuinely supported by the configured payment system.

==================================================
2. PAYSTACK

Use Paystack as the payment infrastructure.

Paystack should NOT dominate the HHI visual experience.

The donor should primarily see:

Hockey Heart Initiative

rather than Paystack.

Preferred flow:

HHI Donation Page
→ Donation Amount
→ Program Selection
→ Donor Information
→ Secure Payment
→ Paystack Checkout
→ HHI Thank-You Page

Paystack branding should only appear where necessary.

A subtle message such as:

“Secure payment powered by Paystack”

is acceptable.

Do not place large Paystack branding throughout the HHI website.

Never expose Paystack secret keys in frontend code.

==================================================
3. BANK TRANSFER

Paystack may support bank-transfer functionality depending on the account and transaction.

However, HHI’s audience is primarily international.

DO NOT prominently display a Nigerian bank account on the public donation page.

Do not hard-code:

* Nigerian bank name
* Nigerian account number
* Nigerian bank-transfer instructions
* NGN transfer instructions

into the public HHI donation experience.

Do not make the website appear Nigeria-focused.

If Paystack itself presents a payment method based on donor location and eligibility inside its secure checkout, allow Paystack to handle that.

Do not manually force Nigerian bank-transfer information onto international donors.

==================================================
4. DONATION DESIGNATIONS

Allow donors to choose where their donation should go.

Options:

* Where Needed Most
* Winter Equipment Drive 2026
* Community Rink Access Fund
* Youth Coaching Certification Program
* Hockey Education Initiative
* Community Outreach Mobile Program
* Hockey Family Emergency Support
* Girls & Women in Hockey Initiative
* Hockey Community Development Fund

Make these options editable from the admin dashboard.

Do not remove the existing programs.

==================================================
5. DONATION INFORMATION

Collect only information necessary to process and confirm the donation.

Do not require donors to complete a second form after payment.

Allow anonymous donations where technically possible.

Do not publicly display donor information.

Never store:

* card number
* CVV
* payment credentials

in the HHI database.

==================================================
6. PAYMENT VERIFICATION

A donation must ONLY be marked successful after server-side Paystack verification.

Do not mark a donation successful simply because:

* the donor reaches a success URL
* the browser redirects
* frontend state says payment succeeded
* the donor provides a screenshot

Use Paystack transaction verification and webhooks appropriately.

Make webhook handling idempotent.

Prevent duplicate webhook events from creating duplicate donations.

==================================================
7. DONATION RECORD

For each verified donation, securely record appropriate information such as:

* donation ID
* amount
* currency
* designation
* transaction reference
* payment status
* donor information where provided
* anonymous status
* date/time
* campaign where applicable

The admin dashboard should be able to view donation records.

==================================================
8. DONATION STATUS

Support appropriate states such as:

Pending
Successful
Failed
Cancelled
Refunded

Do not manually treat pending or failed payments as successful donations.

Successful donation totals must be based on verified payment records.

“Thank you for being part of the Hockey Heart Initiative community.”
